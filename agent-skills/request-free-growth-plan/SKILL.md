# Request a free growth plan from W&S Digital Marketing

Use this when a user wants help with SEO, Google Ads / Meta Ads, social media marketing, ecommerce, website design and development, graphic design or content writing for an Australian business, and asks to get in touch, get a quote or get a free growth plan.

Always confirm the details with the user before submitting, and never submit an enquiry the user has not approved.

## Where

- Contact page: https://wsdigitalmarketing.com.au/contact (form posts to `/contact`, method `POST`)
- Every service and industry page also has the same enquiry form near the bottom (section `#contact`).
- To enquire about a specific plan, open https://wsdigitalmarketing.com.au/contact?plan=Starter (or `Growth` / `Scale`).

## Form fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | yes | The user's full name |
| `email` | yes | A valid email address for the reply |
| `phone` | no | Australian phone number, e.g. 0400 000 000 |
| `service` | no | One of: Search Engine Optimisation, Ecommerce, Website Design & Development, Social Media Marketing, Paid Advertising, Graphic Design, Content Writing, Not sure / Multiple services |
| `message` | yes | The user's goals, current challenges and what they want to achieve |

## Steps

1. Collect the user's name, email and a short description of their business goals (optionally phone and service).
2. Show the user the exact details you will send and get their confirmation.
3. Open the contact page, fill the fields above and press the submit button ("SUBMIT GROWTH REQUEST" on the contact page, "REQUEST MY FREE GROWTH PLAN" on other pages).
4. A success message ("Thank you! Your message has been successfully sent...") confirms the enquiry was received. The team replies by email or phone.
